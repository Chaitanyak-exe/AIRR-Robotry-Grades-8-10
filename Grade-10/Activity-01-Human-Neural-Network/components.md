# Components – Human Neural Network

## Materials

| No. | Component | Quantity | Purpose |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Students | 12 | Act as neurons (3 input + 4 hidden1 + 3 hidden2 + 2 output) |
| 2 | Paper cards | Several | Show neuron names, input values and labels |
| 3 | Marker / pen | 1–2 | Write labels on cards |
| 4 | String / wool or paper arrows | As required | Represent connections between students |
| 5 | Tape | As required | Fix cards or strings in place |
| 6 | Open space / classroom area | 1 | Arrange students in layers comfortably |

## Labels (examples)

- Input students: Student Height, Student Age, Study Hours
- Output students: Pass, Needs More Practice

## Notes

- The exact number of students can be adjusted, but the recommended arrangement for this demonstration is 3 + 4 + 3 + 2 = 12 students.
- No electronic hardware is required for this activity. Do NOT use Arduino, ESP32, sensors, motors, breadboards or similar electronics.
