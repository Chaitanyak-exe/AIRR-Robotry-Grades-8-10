# Components Required

## Hardware Components

| No. | Component | Quantity | Purpose |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Arduino Uno | 1 | Controls the three LEDs |
| 2 | Red LED | 1 | Represents STOP |
| 3 | Yellow LED | 1 | Represents READY |
| 4 | Green LED | 1 | Represents GO |
| 5 | 220Ω Resistor | 3 | Limits current through the LEDs |
| 6 | Breadboard | 1 | Used to build the circuit |
| 7 | Jumper Wires | As required | Used to make connections |
| 8 | USB Cable | 1 | Connects Arduino to the laptop |
| 9 | Laptop | 1 | Used to program the Arduino |

---

## Component Details

### Arduino Uno

Arduino Uno is the main controller used in this activity.

It controls the three LEDs according to the programmed traffic light sequence.

---

### Red LED

The red LED represents the STOP signal.

It is connected to Arduino digital pin D11 through a 220Ω resistor.

---

### Yellow LED

The yellow LED represents the READY signal.

It is connected to Arduino digital pin D10 through a 220Ω resistor.

---

### Green LED

The green LED represents the GO signal.

It is connected to Arduino digital pin D9 through a 220Ω resistor.

---

### 220Ω Resistors

One 220Ω resistor is used with each LED.

The resistor limits the current flowing through the LED.

---

### Breadboard

The breadboard allows the circuit to be assembled without soldering.

---

### Jumper Wires

Jumper wires are used to connect the Arduino pins, LEDs, resistors and GND.

---

### USB Cable

The USB cable connects the Arduino Uno to the laptop.

It is used to upload the program and power the Arduino during the activity.

---

### Laptop

The laptop is used to:

- Write the Arduino program.
- Verify the code.
- Upload the program.
- Troubleshoot the activity.
