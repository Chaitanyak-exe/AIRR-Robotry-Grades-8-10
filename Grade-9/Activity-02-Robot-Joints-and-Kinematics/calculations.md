# Forward Kinematics Calculations

Use the formulas:

```
X = L1 × cos(θ1) + L2 × cos(θ1 + θ2)
Y = L1 × sin(θ1) + L2 × sin(θ1 + θ2)
```

All angles are in degrees. Make sure your calculator is in degree mode.

---

## TEST 1

L1 = 20 cm

L2 = 15 cm

θ1 = 0°

θ2 = 0°

θ1 + θ2 = 0°

Calculate X:

X = 20 × cos(0°) + 15 × cos(0°)

cos(0°) = 1

X = 20 × 1 + 15 × 1 = 20 + 15 = 35 cm

Calculate Y:

Y = 20 × sin(0°) + 15 × sin(0°)

sin(0°) = 0

Y = 20 × 0 + 15 × 0 = 0 cm

---

## TEST 2

L1 = 20 cm

L2 = 15 cm

θ1 = 90°

θ2 = 0°

θ1 + θ2 = 90°

Calculate X:

X = 20 × cos(90°) + 15 × cos(90°)

cos(90°) = 0

X = 20 × 0 + 15 × 0 = 0 cm

Calculate Y:

Y = 20 × sin(90°) + 15 × sin(90°)

sin(90°) = 1

Y = 20 × 1 + 15 × 1 = 20 + 15 = 35 cm

---

## TEST 3

L1 = 20 cm

L2 = 15 cm

θ1 = 45°

θ2 = 45°

θ1 + θ2 = 90°

Calculate X:

X = 20 × cos(45°) + 15 × cos(90°)

cos(45°) = √2 / 2 ≈ 0.70710678

cos(90°) = 0

X = 20 × 0.70710678 + 15 × 0 ≈ 14.1421356 ≈ 14.14 cm

Calculate Y:

Y = 20 × sin(45°) + 15 × sin(90°)

sin(45°) = √2 / 2 ≈ 0.70710678

sin(90°) = 1

Y = 20 × 0.70710678 + 15 × 1 ≈ 14.1421356 + 15 = 29.1421356 ≈ 29.14 cm
