# Components Required

| No. | Component                            | Quantity | Purpose                                |
| --- | ------------------------------------ | -------: | -------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Cardboard strips or ice-cream sticks |        2 | Form the two links of the robot arm    |
| 2   | Paper fasteners / split pins         |        2 | Make the rotating joints between links |
| 3   | Ruler                                |        1 | Measure link lengths and X/Y positions |
| 4   | Protractor                           |        1 | Set and measure joint angles (degrees) |
| 5   | Pencil                               |        1 | Mark holes and draw measurements       |
| 6   | Paper                                |        1 | Work surface and record results        |
| 7   | Scissors                             |        1 | Cut cardboard or trim strips           |
| 8   | Tape or glue                         |        1 | Reinforce links if needed              |
| 9   | Calculator                           |        1 | Calculate X and Y using trig functions |

## Link Dimensions

Link 1 = 20 cm

Link 2 = 15 cm

## Component Details

- Cardboard strips / ice-cream sticks: Provide the rigid links for the model. Use cardboard if you need exact lengths.
- Paper fasteners / split pins: Create simple rotating joints; they let the links turn while staying joined.
- Ruler: Measure link lengths and the final X and Y positions from the base.
- Protractor: Measure and set `θ1` and `θ2` in degrees.
- Pencil and paper: Mark holes, draw the base, and record observations.
- Scissors: Cut cardboard to the required lengths (use sticks if you already have them).
- Tape or glue: Strengthen the link ends if they split; do not block the hole for the fastener.
- Calculator: Use degree-mode trig functions for `cos` and `sin` unless you convert degrees to radians.
