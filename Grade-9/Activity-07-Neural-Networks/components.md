# Components / Requirements

| No. | Component                           | Quantity | Purpose                                        |
| --: | ----------------------------------- | -------: | ---------------------------------------------- |
|   1 | Paper or notebook                   |        1 | Draw the neural-network diagram                |
|   2 | Pencil                              |        1 | Draw and erase as needed                       |
|   3 | Eraser                              |        1 | Correct mistakes                               |
|   4 | Ruler                               |        1 | Draw straight lines for layers and connections |
|   5 | Pen / coloured markers              |        1 | Finalise and label the drawing                 |
|   6 | Optional: Laptop / diagram software |        1 | Draw digitally if preferred                    |

## Notes

- No electronic hardware is required.
- Do not use Arduino, ESP32, sensors, motors, or other electronics for this activity.
