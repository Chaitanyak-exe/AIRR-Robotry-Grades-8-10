# Images

Add only real photographs or screenshots of your completed diagram. Do not add fake images.

Suggested filenames and what they should show:

- `network-drawing.jpg` — a clear photo of the initial pencil drawing on paper.
- `labelled-network.jpg` — the drawing with layer and neuron labels visible.
- `connections.jpg` — a close-up that clearly shows many connection lines and one connection labelled as a weight (for example `w1`).
- `final-result.jpg` — a final neat version of the diagram (pen or marker) showing all labels and the total connection count.

Keep photos well-lit and taken from above so lines and labels are readable.
